# Music bed: picks for the channel owner (no music is generated)

The engine leaves a **music slot**. Download a track from the **YouTube Audio Library** (YouTube Studio > Audio Library; tracks there are cleared for monetised YouTube videos), save it as `music/<story>.mp3`, and render with `--music music/<story>.mp3`. The engine loops it, fades it in and out, ducks it under the voice (sidechain), and sets it **22 dB below the narration** before loudness-normalising the mix to -15 LUFS.

## What to search for in the Audio Library
Use the filters **Genre: Cinematic / Ambient / Classical**, **Mood: Calm / Sad / Romantic / Inspirational**, **Instrument: Piano / Strings / Guitar**, and **Attribution not required** (simpler).

| Story moment | Mood filter | What to listen for |
|---|---|---|
| Quiet openings, kitchen, dawn | Calm | solo felt piano, slow (60-75 BPM), no drums |
| Storm, worry | Dark / Sad | low strings or piano pad, no percussion hits |
| Bond, cozy montage | Romantic / Happy (soft) | acoustic guitar + light piano |
| Letting go, the moral | Sad / Inspirational | piano + cello swell that resolves |

Our own search terms (type in the Audio Library search box): `piano calm`, `lullaby`, `autumn`, `memories`, `gentle`, `tender`, `heartwarming`.

## Rules
- One track per Short and at most 2-3 per long video. Cut on chapter changes.
- Keep a licence record: track name, artist, download date, and the attribution text if one is required (paste it into the description; `SEO_PACK.md` has a reminder).
- Avoid tracks with vocals and tracks with sharp percussion; they fight the narration.
- Do **not** use Creative Commons tracks from outside the Audio Library unless the licence explicitly allows commercial use.

Note: specific track titles are not listed because the Audio Library changes over time and needs a signed-in account to browse. Pick by ear using the filters above.
